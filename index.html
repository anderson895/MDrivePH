    MapDrive

    Drive a car through real cities: free OpenStreetMap 3D buildings, or Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles with a key.

    No sign-up needed. Buildings come from OpenStreetMap with their real mapped heights. They're plain shaded blocks, not photos. Big cities (Makati, BGC, New York, Tokyo) have the most detail. Areas with little mapping look mostly flat.

    Keys are kept only in this browser (localStorage). Restrict your key to your domain in the console.
